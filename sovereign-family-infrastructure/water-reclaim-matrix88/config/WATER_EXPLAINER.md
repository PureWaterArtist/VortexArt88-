## 💧 How the Sieve Works (In Plain English)

Instead of using loud, high-power water pumps, complex electrical filters, or hazardous chemical treatments, this machine copies the exact cell walls and salt-filtering tricks that coastal trees use to survive in muddy ocean water:

1. **The Charcoal Pre-Sponge (Biochar Bed Filter):** Wastewater from family sinks and showers drains down into the first level of a 3D-printed cabinet. This tray is packed with the raw **Activated Carbon Biochar powder** co-produced by our **Termite Gasifier Module**. Because this carbon is incredibly porous, it acts like a massive mechanical magnet, instantly binding and trapping over 99.9% of thick soaps, grease, heavy detergents, and microscopic plastics software-free.
2. **The Molecular Particle Sieve (20nm Graphene Oxide Mesh):** Next, the pre-filtered water drops into a layer lined with ultra-thin **20nm Graphene-Oxide Sheets**. The microscopic holes in these sheets are so small that water molecules can pass through cleanly, but bacteria, viruses, organic chemical residues, and heavy metals are physically blocked at the boundary line, yielding a 94% fluid flux volume recovery.
3. **The Sunlight Disinfector (Solar Parabolic UV-Chute):** The final stage strips out any remaining micro-pathogens without boiling water or using toxic bleach. The filtered water glides slowly down a clear borosilicate glass tube housed inside a curved, shiny parabolic mirror array. Natural ambient sunlight hits the mirror and focuses intense ultraviolet (UV) rays directly through the moving fluid, flash-sterilizing bacteria on contact purely through daylight exposure.
4. **The Air Logic Flush (120-Micron Anti-Clog Tracks):** Over weeks of constant use, lint and hair will gather on the filter faces. Our design includes built-in **120-micron air logic capillaries**. By connecting a short pulse of compressed air from a workshop lines, a burst of air whips across the filter faces to automatically blow away lint obstructions, resetting the sieve tracks cleanly without opening the cabinet.
