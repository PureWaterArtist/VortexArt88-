## 🛠️ 2. Toolless Standard Operating Manual (SOP)

### Phase A: Priming the Carbon Bed Medium
1. Pull out the first-stage carbon bed pack tray (`WAT-BOM-03`). 
2. Pour exactly 1.69 kg of the pure activated carbon biochar powder harvested from Sub-Module 01 directly into the tray mesh. Pack it down evenly to form a tight, uniform, 2-inch deep chemical filtration layer, then slide the tray back into its cabinet seat.

### Phase B: Seating the Sieve Membranes
1. Check that your 20nm absolute graphene-oxide nanopore sheets (`WAT-BOM-02`) are seated completely flat inside the second-stage slide tracks.
2. Tighten the perimeter thumb-screws against the EPDM nitrile rubber compression gaskets (`WAT-BOM-06`) to ensure an airtight seal that forces 100% of incoming water down through the membrane pores.

### Phase C: Daily Gravity-Fed Filtration
1. Position the cabinet directly beneath your household greywater sink or shower exit lines. Let the wastewater drain down the top intake funnel purely via gravity.
2. Ensure the lower borosilicate glass fluid sleeve is placed in direct path alignment under your parabolic concentrating solar reflector array (`WAT-BOM-04`) to maximize natural UV daylight sterilization.

### Phase D: Cleaning and Maintenance Back-Flushes
1. Every 7 days of continuous household operation, connect a brief pulse of compressed air from a manual workshop line to the built-in 120-micron internal air logic capillaries.
2. This pneumatic back-flush will violently whip across the graphene sieve faces, blowing away accumulated hair, skin cells, and lint lines into a waste pocket without requiring you to open the cabinet shell.
