## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

1. **Shell Interlocking:** Snap the modular Grow Tower shell blocks together vertically, validating that the 3-square-foot standing footprint base sits perfectly level.
2. **Peristaltic Core Threading:** Drop the flexible elastomeric tough resin misting tube down the center axis track, ensuring the 120-micron internal air logic capillaries line up un-bent.
3. **Root Cup Packing:** Fill the hexagonal scale grow cups with a blended 35% weight mixture of Sub-Module 01 biochar and local compost slurry to establish the subsoil nutrient bridge.
4. **Hydro-Mist Cycle Check:** Connect the re-claimed water feed lines from Sub-Module 02. Apply a 2.5 Hz pneumatic pulse cycle from your air line to confirm that the internal core throws out a uniform, micro-fine atomized root mist across all growth bays without clogging.
