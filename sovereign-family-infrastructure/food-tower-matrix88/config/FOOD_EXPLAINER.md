## 🛠️ 2. Toolless Standard Operating Manual (SOP)

### Phase A: Packing the Hexagonal Root Pods
1. Mix exactly 35% by weight of the pure activated carbon biochar co-produced by Sub-Module 01 directly into your local organic compost slurry.
2. Pack this porous mixture tightly into the removable grow cups (`FOO-BOM-05`). Press your plant seedlings or root cuttings directly into the carbon core, and slot the cups firmly into the 25-degree gravity-slant grow bays.

### Phase B: Hooking up the Water Logic Lines
1. Connect the clean water exit tubing from Sub-Module 02 straight to the bottom intake manifold of the vertical column tower.
2. Attach your low-power compressed air supply line directly to the multi-chamber distribution manifold (`FOO-BOM-04`). Turn on the pressure regulator to establish the steady 2.5 Hz pneumatic pulse rhythm down the internal 120-micron capillaries.

### Phase C: Monitoring the Internal Hydro-Mist
1. Peep through an open grow bay to verify that the internal peristaltic siphon core is drawing liquid up the 2-meter column and atomizing it through the 0.3mm brass swirl nozzles into a fine, cloud-like root mist.
2. The roots must hang freely inside this humid oxygen void. No electronic timers, water floats, or microcontrollers are required.

### Phase D: Continuous Harvesting and Rotations
1. Clip fresh leafy greens or harvest root crops directly from the tower face as they mature. 
2. When a planting cup is spent, pull the pod out, dump the root-bound biochar matrix straight into your backyard landscaping beds to act as a permanent subsoil nutrient bridge, and pack the cup with a fresh carbon-compost mix to restart the cycle.

## 📡 3. Software-Free Troubleshooting Matrix

| Visual Failure Symptom | Identified Physical Root Cause | Target Correction Protocol (Toolless Bench Action) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plant roots are bone-dry and look withered; no mist inside column.** | Air lines have dropped pressure below 175 kPa, causing the valveless peristaltic siphon to lose its vertical lift. | Check your main pressure line regulator. Adjust the input air feed back to a stable 200 kPa to restore the 2.5 Hz pumping rhythm. |
| **Water pools up and leaks out from the lower column joints.** | Algae, roots, or sediment blocking the 0.3mm brass swirl nozzles, causing fluid build-up. | Unscrew the brass nozzle orifices from the core track, blow a high-pressure air line through the tip to clear sediment, and thread them back into place. |
| **Seedlings look pale or display yellowing leaves after 2 weeks.** | The carbon root matrix lacks trace elements, or water lacks basic organic nutrient salts. | Flush your water loop. Inject a richer blend of organic compost tea or nitrogen-rich liquid manure slurry directly into the Sub-Module 02 clean reservoir. |
