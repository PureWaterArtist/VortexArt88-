## 🛠️ SECTION 1: THE INITIAL POST-PRINT HARVEST & ZERO-SLOP ASSEMBLY

Because these interlocking national flag decals feature an advanced multi-material co-extrusion profile (UV-stabilized Matte ASA top strata + flexible 95A TPU base), you must follow this strict sequential process to ensure flawless, weather-tight field alignment on the vehicle:

### 1. The Sub-35°C Plate Stabilization Cool-Down
*   **Action:** Never attempt to pull or peel a hot decal tile off the print plate the exact second the multi-color run concludes. Allow the print chamber and the spring steel PEI sheet to naturally cool completely below 35°C. This allows the dense elastomeric polymer chains and the rigid ASA flag blocks to stabilize to their hardcoded geometric limits, preventing structural warping or corner-lift.

### 2. The Flat Runway Local-Flex Harvest
*   **Action:** Pull the cooled spring steel sheet off the magnetic bed. Flex the steel sheet downward locally away from the outer edges. The hexagonal tile template will instantly click and pop off the textured face with zero scraping tools, ensuring the sharp, sweeping trailing edges of the falcon-wing airfoil bezel remain pristine and undamaged.

### 3. The Countersunk Magnet Insertion
*   **Action:** Sourcing standard, high-strength **10mm x 2mm N52 Neodymium disc magnets**. Before interlocking the puzzle pieces, turn the tile over with the tree-frog suction gasket grid facing upward. Align an individual magnet disc directly over each of the six countersunk cylindrical pockets and press down firmly using flat thumb pressure. Because the flexible TPU compartment is hardcoded with a precise **0.20mm tolerance compression-fit reduction ring**, the walls will stretch open locally and snap completely over the rim of the magnet, locking it inside a permanent physical collar without any messy shop adhesives.

### 4. The Zero-Slop Interlocking Grid Assembly
*   **Action:** Lay your harvested tiles flat on a clean table. Slide the male re-entrant jigsaw suture pin of Tile A straight into the female keyway slot of Tile B. 
*   **The Mechanical Fit:** Push horizontally until the teeth click home. Because our parametric architecture hardcodes a strict, absolute **0.15mm clearance gap** that remains locked independent of custom tile scaling, the puzzle pieces will join with perfect zero-slop precision. The integrated scallop fluid drainage channels will line up seamlessly, creating a flat, water-tight mosaic banner panel.

## 🔬 SECTION 2: PAINT-SAFE FIELD APPLICATION & FLEET MAINTENANCE

To guarantee absolute paint protection and ensure the assembled graphics hold a permanent downforce lock at 75 MPH highway speeds, execute this rigid field standard operating procedure:

### 1. The Sheet-Metal Surface Audit
*   **Action:** Before snapping the assembled decal matrix onto a truck door or tailgate, wipe the vehicle's steel panel clean using a soft microfiber cloth and a standard alcohol-free automotive detail spray. Ensure the paint surface is completely free of loose road grit, thick mud, or caked salt. 

### 2. The Tree-Frog Vacuum Engagement
*   **Action:** Hold the assembled interlocking flag matrix firmly by the edges and press it flatly against the clean steel panel. The powerful N52 Neodymium magnets will instantly pull the tile grid flush against the car. Press your hand firmly over the center of each tile, smoothing outward toward the parabolic airfoil edges. This action compresses the soft outer perimeter gasket loop of the flexible TPU base, trapping a localized micro-vacuum inside the pre-molded hexagonal suction cups to block road dust sandpaper effects completely.

### 3. The Single-Direction Fluid Flush Routine
*   **Action:** When the vehicle graphics become coated in highway road salt, bugs, or driving mud, wash the tiles during your normal truck wash cycle. **Always spray or wipe the tiles in a single direction matching the vehicle's airflow (from the front of the truck toward the rear).** This allows water to run smoothly down the natural lay of the scallop fluid drainage gutters, instantly flushing out all trapped road grime by gravity. Never push a high-pressure pressure washer nozzle directly underneath the thinned 0.4mm airfoil edge, as this fights the downforce vector and can break the micro-vacuum seal.
