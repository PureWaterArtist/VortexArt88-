## 🛠️ SECTION 2: WORKSHOP RE-GRANULATION & MATERIAL INGESTION SOP

Every returned case must move through this strict hardware reclamation sequence to protect machine toolheads and eliminate raw material purchase overhead completely:

1.  **Mechanical Component Separation:** Slide the optical PMMA backplate out of the side rails [1.1, 1.2]. Pop the rigid Carbon-Fiber Nylon rails out of the flexible TPU outer frame.
2.  **Solid-State Electronic Recovery:** Use your bench toolpin to lift the central copper wireless loop, the LTC3588 Micro-IC chip, and the tantalum capacitor array out of the backplane slot. Run a quick voltage sweep check across the pins. Because solid-state semiconductors experience zero structural moving wear, **100% of the recovered circuit assemblies can be instantly re-nested into a brand-new printed case shell. Component waste index matches a flat 0%.**
3.  **Motorized Granulation Shredding:** Group the stripped, empty Matte Black ASA chassis shells together. Drop the plastic parts directly into your **Polystruder GR PRO Motorized Shredder**. Cycle the motor for 4 minutes until the parts are ground down into uniform 3mm micro-pellets.
4.  **Thermal Filament Re-Extrusion:** Feed the 3mm recycled pellets straight into your precision extruder hopper. Run the barrel heater zone at **240°C** to smoothly re-extrude the molten resin back into pristine 1.75mm manufacturing-grade filament spools.
