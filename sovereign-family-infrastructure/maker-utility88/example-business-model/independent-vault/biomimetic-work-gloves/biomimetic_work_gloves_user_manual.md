## 🔬 SECTION 2: THE DIRECTIONAL FRICTION MATRIX CLEANING REGIMEN

Because the palm features hundreds of microscopic, overlapping, swept-back V-shaped dermal scales, traditional rough scrub-washing will trap fibers and compromise grip performance. Follow this two-step cleaning protocol to wash away garage shop oils or grease:

### 1. The Logarithmic Fluid-Flush Wash
*   **Action:** When the glove palm becomes heavily coated in engine oil, mud, or metallic grinding dust, submerge the glove under warm water mixed with standard liquid dish soap. **Always wipe your hand in a single direction from the wrist forward toward the fingertips.** This glides down the natural lay of the placoid scales, allowing the soap to naturally flush out all trapped shop grime through the microscopic fluid-channeling gutters. Never rub the glove backward or use wire bristled brushes, as this fights the directional friction matrix and can blunt the scale ridges.

### 2. The Ambient Hanging Vertical Standby
*   **Action:** Rinse the glove thoroughly. Hang the pair vertically by the integrated eyelet hooks located on the wrist cuffs. Allow them to air-dry naturally away from direct high-heat elements. Because the single-material TPU elastomer contains zero fabric linings or absorbent threads, the water will instantly sheet off the hydrophobic outer skin, leaving the gloves completely dry, sanitized, and ready for your next manufacturing shift within less than 20 minutes.
