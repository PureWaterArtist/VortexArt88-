Because this vehicle is engineered with a Modular Toolless Disassembly Architecture, you do not use metal thread screws, chemical glues, or permanent adhesives. The entire frame connects through pure code-driven geometric interlocks.

### 1. Electronics Drop-In Snap Ingestion
*   **Action:** Take your metal-geared 17g micro steering servo. Drop it vertically into the front frame socket. Press down firmly until the high-rebound TPU spring tab clicks shut over the mounting ears.

### 2. Symmetrical Cranial Suture Interlock

### 3. Solid-State Telescoping Axle Insertion
*   **Action:** Take your 3D-printed Carbon-Fiber Nylon Outer Female Axle Cups. Press-fit their bases directly over the metal output pins of your differentials.
*   **The Telescoping Match:** Insert the 3.0mm solid square tips of your Polypropylene Male Axle Shafts straight inside the 3.2mm square internal channels of the female cups. Slide the outer round ends of the male shafts through the wheel hub bearings and lock the wheel pins in place. 
*   **The Flex Advantage:** When the car accelerates, the flat square faces lock against each other to transfer 100% of the motor's rotational torque. When the truck takes a deep jump and the suspension expands, the male shafts smoothly slide out of the female cups by up to 4.0mm on the fly, entirely absorbing suspension travel with zero pulling force or stress on your layer lines.
