# 🏛️ Project Proposal Grant Abstract: Distributed Micro-Foundry Infrastructure

### A. PROJECT OBJECTIVE & STRATEGIC STATE ALIGNMENT
Matrix Biomimetic Utilities, LLC is establishing an automated, localized Circular Micro-Foundry Pilot Unit in Westland, Michigan. This facility utilizes advanced additive engineering and localized mechanical reclamation systems to manufacture multi-layered, scale-invariant infrastructure components entirely from post-consumer recycled plastics. 

Our core portfolio directly addresses the strategic sustainability pillars of the **Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE)** and the **MEDC**:
1.  **The Solar-Scale Engine:** A 24-hour energy harvesting asset combining angle-invariant micro-prisms with solid-state Seebeck thermoelectric harvesters to provide decentralized, parallel grid resilience that operates day and night without centralized infrastructure or glass sheet dependencies.
2.  **The Strata-Flow Tree:** A passive, gravity-fed greywater diversion unit leveraging centrifugal fluid dynamics and anti-bacterial sharkskin textures to purify domestic drainage on-site, directly supporting Great Lakes watershed water conservation mandates.

### B. TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY & MATERIAL SUSTAINABILITY
The entire structural ecosystem is fully mature, open-source, and version-controlled via private parametric CAD engines and C++ automation firmware architectures. To maximize environmental alignment, our flagship product utilizes optical-grade **PMMA (Acrylic)** for its protective lens and light-trapping face. Unlike traditional plastics, PMMA is 100% BPA-free, non-toxic, and undergoes clean thermal depolymerization, allowing it to be recycled and re-printed infinitely across generations without losing optical clarity or yellowing under the sun. By executing production through a redundant cell of enclosed high-speed CoreXY print engines running specialized, watertight layer configurations, the facility operates with absolute zero raw material waste.

### C. FUNDING MECHANICS & REGIONAL IMPACT
Rather than relying on traditional debt-heavy commercial financing, this infrastructure project utilizes a 2-to-1 matching framework. We are raising our initial capital runway through non-equity community pre-sales to prove localized market viability. Grant funding will be allocated 100% toward matching those pre-orders via the acquisition of physical capital machinery (motorized shredders, desktop filament extruders, and enclosed production units). By operating under a Lifetime Circular Warranty, this project creates an active, community-driven reclamation network. Worn or retired units are retrieved via prepaid logistics loops, shredded, re-compounded, and remanufactured locally—establishing a robust, scalable template for regional small-business circular economics within the State of Michigan.
