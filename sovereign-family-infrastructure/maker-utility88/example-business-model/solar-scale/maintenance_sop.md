# 🔧 Machine Maintenance & Preventative Calibration Log
**Operational Scope:** Bambu Lab P1S Production Farm Calibration Schedules  

To maintain the strict dimensional tolerances required for toolless snap-fit hexagonal tessellation, technicians must execute these maintenance schedules every 500 active print hours.

### 1. Extruder Gear and Carbon-Fiber Clears (Every 500 Hours)
*   **SOP:** Open the toolhead assembly. Clean the direct-drive hardened steel dual gears using an industrial wire brush to remove compressed polymer dust from the recycled filament runs.
*   **Nozzle Purge:** Swap the 0.4mm stainless steel nozzle for a hardened steel nozzle matrix when running heavy cycles of composite conductive carbon-fiber or PVDF filaments to prevent orifice ovaling.

### 2. Axis Linear Rail Lubrication (Monthly Internal Cadence)
*   **SOP:** Clean the carbon fiber X-axis linear rods using Isopropyl Alcohol (99% purity) and a lint-free microfiber cloth. Lubricate the Y-axis steel linear rails and Z-axis lead screws using high-purity synthetic grease (Magnalube-G).
*   **Resonance Tuning:** Re-run the automated software input-shaping and structural frequency calibration routines to completely eliminate layer ghosting and ensure perfect optical clarity on the PMMA prism faces.
