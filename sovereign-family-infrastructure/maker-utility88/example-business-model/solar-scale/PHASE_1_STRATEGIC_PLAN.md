## 📈 SECTION 2: CRITICAL REAL-WORLD RECONCILIATION DATA

Lenders, state grant committees, and corporate risk managers evaluate projects based on real-world constraints. Keep these three structural facts locked into your planning logs:

1.  **The Grant Reimbursement Curve:** Michigan state-backed funding mechanisms (such as EGLE or MEDC infrastructure grants) operate strictly on a **reimbursement schedule**. The state does not provide upfront cash to buy machinery. You must show the accelerator board a clear log of customer pre-orders or alternative non-debt capital to purchase the hardware loops upfront, after which the state issues a dollar-for-dollar cash check upon verification of operations inside Wayne County.
2.  **Multi-Material Slicing Thresholds:** Co-extruding three radically distinct polymer profiles (rigid ASA, flexible rubberized TPU, and highly transparent optical PMMA Acrylic) inside a single, continuous print head causes immediate nozzle clogging and cross-contamination layer failures on basic hardware. The operational planning phase must focus on a **3-component snap-fit assembly layout** to keep print farm defect curves low.
3.  **The Operational Timeline Window:** The intake, MNDA signing, voucher approval, and prototype production cycles across Michigan state-backed networks require an active operational runway of **90 to 180 days**. Maintaining a strict planning posture protects your cash flow from drying up during these administrative windows.
