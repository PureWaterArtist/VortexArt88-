# 🌿 Environmental Safety, Storage, and Battery Disposal Compliance
**Operational Scope:** Local and Federal Safety Regulation Management  
**Target Jurisdiction:** City of Westland, MI 48185 / Wayne County Environmental Services  

### 1. Chemical Storage Infrastructure
*   **Protocol:** Raw lithium-polymer (Li-Po) cells, Bismuth Telluride modules, and electronic subassemblies must be stored inside a heavy-duty, OSHA-compliant explosion-proof fire cabinet.
*   **Climate Management:** Ambient storage vault humidity must remain strictly $\le 20\%$ using active desiccant cartridges to prevent metallic corrosion on connection leads.

### 2. Hardware Recycling and E-Waste Sourcing Lines
*   **Protocol:** While 100% of our printed structural polymer casings are mechanically recycled on-site through the Polystruder shredder, damaged monocrystalline silicon solar shards and defective microcontroller silicon boards cannot be processed in-house.
*   **Fulfillment:** Defective electronics must be collected inside a dedicated hazardous material bin and deposited bi-annually at the **Wayne County Household Hazardous Waste Collection Depot** (or processed via certified B2B e-waste reclamation partners) to maintain absolute environmental alignment with our brand's zero-landfill manifesto.
