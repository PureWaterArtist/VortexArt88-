## 🔌 2. SAFE ELECTRICAL SEGREGATION & FIRE PREVENTION

Standard residential garages run on a single, shared 15-amp circuit breaker loop. Running six high-heat print heads, heated beds, and a melting extruder line simultaneously on that line will instantly trip your household breaker. 

### 🔹 The 30-Amp Service Drop (Sourced via Local Electrician Counter)
*   **The Layout:** Run a dedicated **240V, 30-amp independent service line** directly from your primary household electrical box straight to a new sub-panel mounted on your garage back wall. 
*   **The Balancing:** Split this 30-amp service line into three independent 20-amp, 120V household branch circuits:
    *   *Circuit 1 (Manufacturing Wall Base):* Services Print Engines 1 through 6.
    *   *Circuit 2 (Reclaim Bench Base):* Services the Motorized Shredder and melting extruder motor.
    *   *Circuit 3 (Lighting & Logistics Hub):* Services your high-lumen overhead light grid and label stations.
*   **The Result:** Clean, un-interrupted power distribution across all high-wattage hardware components without ever blinking a single light in your kitchen.

### 🔹 Fail-Safe Automation & Suppression (The Household Protection Shield)
*   **Automatic Fire Suppression:** Mount a **Blazecut Automatic Fire Suppression System Hose (Model T200E)** or a **Jofire 2kg Overhead Auto-Extinguisher Ball** inside the upper roof ceiling of each OSB printer cabinet. If an internal power supply short-circuit ever occurs while you are sleeping, the ambient heat will instantly rupture the pressurized tube or ball, automatically flooding the isolated cabinet with clean, skin-safe FM-200 gas to snuff out the flame in under 3 seconds before it can ever breach the wood shell.
*   **Smart Electrical Kill-Switches:** Plug your print rack power strips into a central **Heavy-Duty Smart WiFi Breaker Outlet (such as a 15A Kasa Smart Plug)** connected straight to your phone. If your smartphone telemetry dashboard flags an unexpected temperature spike, you can kill the raw electrical line to the entire rack remotely from your bed with a single swipe.

## 🌬️ 3. CONTINUOUS ODOR-FREE NEGATIVE-PRESSURE VENTILATION

To ensure your home and your neighbors never smell superheated rubber or melting elastomers during heavy overnight manufacturing drops, the air-exchange network is engineered on a closed **Negative-Pressure Scrubber Track**.

*   **The Extraction Manifold:** Run a solid 4-inch rigid aluminum ducting line across the back top ceiling of your OSB printer cabinets. Every individual cabinet connects to this master trunk via an adjustable slider damper gate.
*   **The Air-Scrubber Core:** Connect the rigid duct manifold straight to **3x VIVOSUN 4-Inch Inline Duct Fans with Activated Charcoal Canister Filters** (Sourced directly from the Livonia Grainger counter under Catalog Combo Kit).
*   **The Negative-Pressure Action:** The fans pull a continuous vacuum, drawing all internal cabinet heat and emissions *out* of the printer bays. The air is forced through 2-inch thick beds of virgin Australian activated charcoal, completely scrubbing out all micro-particulates and organic compounds.
*   **The Exterior Vent Path:** Guide the clean, scrubbed, odor-free air output line straight through a dedicated 4-inch dryer-vent wall hood installed out through a side garage window panel or an exterior soffit rail. The garage air remains completely clean, cool, and fresh 24 hours a day.
