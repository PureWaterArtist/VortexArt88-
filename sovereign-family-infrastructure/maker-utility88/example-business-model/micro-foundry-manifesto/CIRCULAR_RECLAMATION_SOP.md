## 🛠️ 2. TOOLLESS BENCH DISASSEMBLY & SORTING MANIFEST

Once the postal carrier delivers the retrieval package to your Westland workshop door, the unboxing and multi-material separation protocol must follow this exact 3-minute sequence to keep components from being cross-contaminated:

### Step 1: Mechanical Framework Splitting
*   **Action:** Take the returned asset envelope. Slice the paper seal and drop the chassis onto your clean workspace bench.
*   **The Protocol:** Exploit our hard-coded **Modular Toolless Disassembly Architecture**. For the smart case, slide the clear PMMA backplate out of its tongue-and-groove tracks. For the RC truck, use a 3D-printed punch pin to push out the interlocking cranial vertebrae rods. The flexible TPU bumper hull will separate cleanly from the rigid Carbon-Fiber Nylon inner spine with zero hand tools.

### Step 2: Solid-State Semiconductor Recovery
*   **Action:** Use a non-conductive plastic pry tool to lift the central copper wireless transmission loop, the LTC3588 Power Management Micro-IC chip, and the tantalum capacitor array out of the cored backplane slots.
*   **The Quality Check:** Connect the circuit leads to an active bench oscilloscope to run a quick 5V voltage sweep check. Because solid-state microchips and copper loops experience **zero mechanical moving parts to wear out or fatigue**, 100% of these electronics cores can be immediately re-nested directly into a brand-new printed case shell. **Your electronic component waste index matches a flat 0%.**

### Step 3: Polymer Material Bin Sorting
*   **Action:** Sort the completely stripped, empty plastic shells into their designated color-coded workspace recycling bins to keep polymers from mixing during melting loops:
    *   **Bin Alpha (Black):** Rigid, high-stiffness Carbon-Fiber Nylon (PA-CF) rails and RC motor spines.
    *   **Bin Beta (Clear):** Optical-grade polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA Acrylic) prism backplates.
    *   **Bin Gamma (Charcoal):** High-rebound flexible Thermoplastic Polyurethane (TPU) bumpers and outer exoskeleton hulls.
    *   **Bin Delta (Natural):** Ductile, fatigue-resistant Polypropylene (PP) sliding telescoping drive shafts.

## 🔬 3. MOTORIZED SHREDDING & RESIN RE-EXTRUSION SPECS

To turn the sorted plastic shells back into raw engineering spools without ruining your printer's toolheads, follow these exact re-compounding steps:

1.  **Granulation Shredding:** Empty a single polymer bin category (e.g., 20 retrieved Matte Black ASA or TPU frames) directly into the hopper of your **Polystruder GR PRO Motorized Shredder**. Turn on the motor and let the blades run for exactly 4 minutes until the parts are ground down into uniform **3mm micro-pellets**.
2.  **De-Humidification Baking:** Pour the 3mm micro-pellets flat onto a stainless steel baking tray. Place the tray inside your active material drying oven to cook out trapped moisture before extrusion:
    *   *ASA / TPU Pellets:* Bake at **65°C for 4 hours**.
    *   *Carbon-Fiber Nylon Pellets:* Bake at **80°C for 6 hours** to drop internal moisture content below 0.05% to prevent foaming defects during printing.
3.  **Thermal Filament Re-Extrusion:** Feed the dried pellets into your precision twin-screw filament extruder hopper. Run the barrel heater zones at your material's precise target melting temperature:
    *   *TPU Profiles:* Melt and extrude at **235°C** nozzle velocity.
    *   *ASA/Acrylic Profiles:* Melt and extrude at **245°C** nozzle velocity.
    *   *PA-CF Profiles:* Melt and extrude at **285°C** nozzle velocity.
4.  **Diameter Calibration Spooling:** Guide the extruded molten plastic strand through an active dual-axis laser diameter gauge to ensure the filament holds a strict **1.75mm ± 0.03mm dimensional accuracy window**. Wind the calibrated filament directly onto an empty cardboard spool.
