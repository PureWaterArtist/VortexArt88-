# 🏛️ Micro-Foundry Operations SOP: Zero-Stress Autonomous Manufacturing
**System Core:** Automated Toolpath Dispatching, Cool-Bed Component Release, and Dedicated Engine Tracking  
**Target Resource Draw:** Less than 30 Minutes of Manual Workbench Interaction Per Day  
**Operational Posture:** 100% Hands-Off Autonomy / Zero-Babysitting Processing Loops  

---

## 📈 1. THE TWICE-DAILY FULFILLMENT CADENCE

### 🔹 07:00 AM Morning Intake Run (15 Minutes)
1.  **Extract:** Open the acoustic sound cabinet enclosures. Ensure hotend and heated bed telemetry read safely below 35°C. Flex the spring-steel textured PEI sheets slightly to naturally release the printed rubber upper frames and soles straight into assembly bins.
2.  **Snap:** Align the longitudinal male splines of Layer 1 with the female tracks of Layer 2. Slide the shell forward until it locks cleanly into the vertical rear heel-snap latch toggle. Slide the porous velvet insert liner flat inside the ankle opening cavity. (Total processing allocation: 15 seconds per finished pair).
3.  **Ship:** Flat-pack the footwear inside our branded Kraft cardboard shoeboxes. Paste on the pre-compiled ShipStation shipping labels. Deposit the items at the residential porch collection station for automated daily postal pickup loops.

### 🔹 09:30 PM Evening Dispatch Run (15 Minutes)
1.  **Condition:** Execute a quick, 5-second uniform mist spray of liquid PVP adhesive across all 6 PEI sheets to secure zero-warp flat-bed adhesion.
2.  **Dispatch:** Fire up the cloud slicing server dashboard. Select the supportless footwear project toolpaths, and wirelessly dispatch the G-code execution commands across all 6 dedicated print engines simultaneously.
3.  **Seal:** Lock the heavy acoustic cabinet doors flat. Verify that the negative-pressure carbon air filter arrays turn on cleanly to isolate processing heat and rubber smells. Walk completely away from the shop floor.

## 🛡️ 2. THE WORKSHOP INFRASTRUCTURE MONOPOLY COVENANTS

*   **Dedicated Material Tracking Nodes:** To entirely eliminate nozzle contamination, line purging delays, and color-bleeding tracking defects, the 6-printer fleet operates on an ironclad segregation framework:
    *   *Core 1 & Core 2:* Hard-locked exclusively to Layer 1 (85A TPU Soft Uppers).
    *   *Core 3 & Core 4:* Hard-locked exclusively to Layer 2 (95A TPU Cushion Soles).
    *   *Core 5 & Core 6:* Hard-locked exclusively to Layer 3 (80A TPU Porous Insoles).
*   **The Quarterly 60-Minute Batch Audit:** On the first Saturday morning of every quarter, operations enter preventive maintenance cycles. Both co-founders spend 60 minutes wiping linear rods with light synthetic oil, validating belt tension resonance offsets, and clearing the motorized granulator shredder blades. This ensures zero operational failures during active direct-to-consumer online flash drops.
