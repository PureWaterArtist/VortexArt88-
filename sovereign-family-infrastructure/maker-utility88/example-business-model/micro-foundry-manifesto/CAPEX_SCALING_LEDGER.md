If a legacy factory faces an unexpected macroeconomic contraction, its multi-thousand-dollar steel injection molds and single-purpose stamping machines hold a secondary market resale value of exactly **$0.00**—they are custom shapes that cannot be repurposed, turning them into immediate toxic balance sheet liabilities.

*   **The Foundry Shield:** 100% of your scaling capital is locked directly into **highly versatile, standard CoreXY print cores and precision polymer compounding hardware**. These tools are open-market commodities with constant, massive global demand across the engineering, aerospace, medical, and hobby sectors.
*   **The 14-Day Capital Clawback:** If an absolute worst-case market contraction hits your primary contract client, you do not face corporate bankruptcy. Your industrial machinery assets can be listed and fully liquidated on secondary equipment marketplaces within 14 days, recovering up to **75% to 85% of your total principal capital value** instantly to completely de-risk your personal treasury.
