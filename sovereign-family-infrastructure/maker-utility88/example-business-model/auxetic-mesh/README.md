*   **Trabecular Bone Shock Distribution:** The interior core mimics the micro-architecture of equine hoof structures. When hitting sharp potholes, curbs, or glass debris, the geometric cells compress and distribute impact forces evenly across the entire structural diameter of the wheel hub, shielding the vehicle's bearings from sudden shear spikes.
