## TERMS AND CONDITIONS

### 1. Definition of Confidential Information
For purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" shall include all information or material that has or could have commercial value or other utility in the business in which the disclosing Party is engaged. 
*   **For MBU, Confidential Information explicitly includes:** All source code, compiled `.gcode` lines, machine-readable `.json` profiles, parametric models, circuit schematics, multi-phase harvesting metrics, and hardware tolerances hosted inside its private digital vaults.
*   **For Investor, Confidential Information explicitly includes:** All corporate financial metrics, internal facility failure/scrap logs, supply chain distribution routes, marketing margins, and procurement cost structures.

### 2. General Obligations and Restrictions
The Receiving Party shall hold and maintain the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in strictest confidence for the sole and exclusive benefit of the Disclosing Party. 
*   **Use Limitations:** The Receiving Party shall restrict access to Confidential Information to its employees, legal counsel, and facility engineers who have a clear, documented "need to know" to fulfill the Authorized Purpose, and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those contained herein.
*   **Reproduction Prohibitions:** Neither Party shall copy, reverse-engineer, decompile, or synthesize any mechanical models, code matrices, or physical subassemblies disclosed under this framework without the prior express written authorization of the Disclosing Party.

### 3. Exclusions from Confidentiality
The obligations under this Agreement shall not apply to any information that:
1. Is or becomes publicly known through no wrongful act or breach of covenant by the Receiving Party.
2. Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction and without breach of any confidentiality obligation.
3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information, as proven by written corporate records.

### 4. Compelled Disclosure and State Exemptions
If a Receiving Party is legally compelled by court order, subpoena, or administrative mandate to disclose any of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall provide immediate written notice to the Disclosing Party so that a protective order may be sought. 

In direct alignment with the State of Michigan's **Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)**, any data submitted under matching grant applications (MEDC/EGLE NextCycle) shall be aggressively marked under the **Confidential Trade Secret Exemption** to prevent public visibility or corporate data scraping.

### 5. Term, Longevity, and Right of Return
The confidentiality obligations established under this Agreement shall remain in full force for a period of **five (5) years** from the Effective Date, or for as long as the Proprietary Infrastructure Assets retain independent commercial value as trade secrets, whichever duration is longer. 

Upon the written request of the Disclosing Party, or immediately upon the dissolution of discussions regarding the Authorized Purpose, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all physical prototypes, documents, and digital read-access tokens containing Confidential Information, certifying such destruction in writing within seven (7) business days.

### 6. Definite Prior Art and IP Sovereignty
The Investor explicitly acknowledges and agrees that all ownership, copyright, patent, trademark, and trade secret rights in MBU’s private repository remain exclusively vested in Matrix Biomimetic Utilities, LLC. 

Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted as granting any implied license or transfer of intellectual property rights. MBU retains absolute, unassailable sovereignty over its codebases and structural designs.

### 7. Equitable Remedies and Breach Relief
The Parties acknowledge that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement will cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. 

Accordingly, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief and specific performance in a court of competent jurisdiction to enforce the covenants herein, without the necessity of posting a bond, in addition to recovering reasonable attorney's fees, accounting costs, and proven financial damages.

### 8. Governing Law and Severability
This Agreement shall be governed, construed, and enforced in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Michigan, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any legal action, suit, or proceeding arising under or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located within **Wayne County, Michigan**.
