# 🏛️ Biomimetic Reclaimable Vape Enclosure Node
**Enterprise Core:** High-Integrity R&D Prototype & Limited-Edition Hardware Housing
**Material Safety Manifest:** 100% BPA-Free Circular PMMA (Acrylic) & Tactile Recycled ASA
**Project Repository Location:** /mkx-vape-core/README.md

Traditional disposable vaporizer housings and cartridge casings are built on non-recyclable, old-world assembly paradigms. 

Once components are sealed using permanent factory glues, the entire architecture becomes a legal and mechanical dead end. If an automated assembly line experiences microscopic structural defects, battery shelf-life expiration, internal short circuits, or cosmetic scratches during high-speed automated machine filling, the entire unit—including its expensive concentrate oil—must be written off. This creates massive material scrap waste, dangerous lithium battery hazards, and steep state compliance disposal fees.

**The Matrix Modular Vape Enclosure shatters this bottleneck.** We replace single-use, glued tubes with a 2-part snap-fit shell modeled directly after **the protective, shock-absorbing geometric structures of cellular orange peels (citrus exocarp)**. By using a glue-free mechanical snap retention gasket, this architecture separates the sensitive internal battery cell from the oil reservoir. This enables safe component extraction and complete material reclamation during development and limited pilot stages without compromising product safety thresholds.

Standard vape casings rely on permanent adhesive bounds that make material separation impossible. The Matrix shell uses elastic polymer memory to isolate components, providing a clean testing base for hardware evolution.

```text
└── High Compliance Waste Overhead    └── Sustainable Thermal Depolymerization Core
```

### Biomimetic Manufacturing Subsystems:
*   **The Shock-Absorbing Exocarp Skin:** The outer housing features vertical, micro-ribbed protective reinforcement ridges modeled after citrus skin. This structure absorbs drops onto hard factory concrete floors, protecting the fragile glass cartridge and internal circuit boards.
*   **The Glue-Free TPU Retention Gasket:** High-elasticity split retention rings printed out of high-rebound TPU. This gasket expands to receive components during assembly and clicks shut, applying a secure mechanical interference hold without chemical glues.
*   **The Component Extraction Window:** A precision-cut side access window that allows technicians to depress the internal flexible retention tabs using a standard tool pin, sliding internal components out safely if a QA circuit test fails.
