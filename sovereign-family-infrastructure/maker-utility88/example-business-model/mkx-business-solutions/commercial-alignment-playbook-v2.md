### A. The Production Labor Resolution (Automated Bed Clearing)
*   **The Constraint:** Running a 3-printer farm at high capacity requires manual operator intervention at unpredictable hours to clear build plates and strip layer lines, creating an intense labor bottleneck.
*   **The Solution:** All parts are engineered with completely flat, supportless base geometry to print directly on textured PEI spring-steel sheets. By utilizing a **0.4mm Hardened Steel Nozzle Matrix**, parts naturally self-release the exact second the bed drops below 35°C. Manual workspace interaction is throttled back to a single 15-minute facility clearance run every 24 hours.

### B. Material Purity, Warping, and Shrinkage Controls
*   **The Constraint:** High-temperature engineering polymers like weatherproof ASA and chemical-resistant Polypropylene (PP) experience severe thermal contraction shrinkage (1.4% to 2.2%) and emit volatile outgasses, leading to layer delamination, bed warping, and inner radius corner rounding.
*   **The Solution:** Our parametric scripts insert an expanded **+0.22mm tolerance safety offset** and specialized **teardrop corner clearing slots** to prevent nozzle rounding. The slicing profiles lock cooling fans to a strict **0% profile** to maintain absolute heat uniformity across the bed, while the bottom faces of the models integrate shallow **0.5mm micro-ventilation cross-ribs** to let expanding outgasses escape flat plates safely, ensuring a warp-free base.

### C. The METRC Regulatory Strategy (The Phase 1 Focus Pivot)
*   **The Constraint:** Attempting to manually disassemble defective electronic vape devices to slide oil reservoirs out directly on an active automated filling line triggers extreme state-mandated METRC software compliance audits, inventory diversion liabilities, and product sterility risks.
*   **The Solution:** To guarantee absolute safety, **the company completely freezes all regulated product lines on Day One.** The Air Scrubber, the Greywater Tree, and the Vape Enclosure are fully benched inside your private repository files. 100% of your farm's initial manufacturing capacity is strictly restricted to **Product Variant 5: The MKX Modular Retail Matrix Display Tray**. Because a display tray functions as a purely structural, non-regulated point-of-sale asset, it circumvents all state packaging inspections and METRC tracking checkpoints, making it the ultimate risk-free commercial pilot line to validate print farm turnaround velocities.
