# 🔧 Reusable Transit Packaging Shield — Operational SOP & Quality Control

**Operational Target:** High-Velocity Slide-Lock Core Assemblies

## 🛠️ SECTION 1: THE RE-ENGINEERED SLIDE-LOCK ASSEMBLY PROTOCOL

Because the transport sleeve utilizes a **2-piece mechanical slide-lock configuration** to bypass chemical polymer bonding limits, cleanroom assembly must follow this sequence exactly:

1.  **Chassis Extraction:** Remove the cooled, matte black ASA exoskeleton shell base off the PEI build sheet once temperatures drop below 35°C to avoid geometric bending.
2.  **Core Ingestion Loop:** Take the thin-walled, flexible TPU internal spring finger sleeve core insert. Align it with the top entry face of the ASA base chassis.
3.  **Mechanical Slide-Lock:** Compress the flexible TPU core slightly and slide it straight down into the internal chamber of the ASA exoskeleton housing [1.1, 1.2]. The hard-coded **0.15mm interference air gap tolerance** ensures the sleeve presses tightly against the perimeter walls, locking mechanically into place without glues, thermal welding, or fasteners.
4.  **Cap Interface Verification:** Spin a standard multi-use child-resistant locking cap onto the top twist flange groove to confirm the locking tracks rotate smoothly with zero mechanical binding.

## 📊 SECTION 2: LABORATORY EXTRACTION AND DROP ROAD TESTS

### The Fully Loaded Crate Transit Shock Test
*   **SOP:** Pack a standard industrial transit container to maximum capacity with 48 completed, fully assembled Matrix Transit Packaging Shields, each loaded with a solid steel testing cylinder weight. Raise the fully loaded crate to a height of **1.8 meters (approx. 6 feet)** over a solid concrete workshop floor. Release the crate to force a corner impact landing.
*   **Evaluation Parameters:** Open the transport crate. The interlocking vertical alignment ribs on the outer faces of the ASA shells must absorb the impact energy cleanly, showing **zero splitting, zero structural flattening, and zero layer delamination**. Slide the internal flexible TPU finger sleeves out of the housings; they must retain absolute elastic structural rebound memory, holding their friction grip parameters with zero displacement. Any structural fracture triggers an automatic batch **FAILURE**, sending the entire lot straight to the shredder matrix to be reprocessed.
