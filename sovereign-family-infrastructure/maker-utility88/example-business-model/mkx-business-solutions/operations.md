# 🔧 Shelled MKX Synergy Display Tray — Production & QA Framework
**System Version:** 2.0.0-Shelled Core  
**Operational Target:** High-Velocity Supportless Component Output  

---

## 🛠️ SECTION 1: THE RE-ENGINEERED ASSEMBLY PROTOCOL

Because the internal cavity beneath the tray floor is entirely hollowed out to meet the **85-gram production limit**, assembly actions must utilize targeted, localized insertion pressure to prevent chassis flexing:

1.  **Chassis Extraction:** Peel the cooled 85g ASA chassis plate off the spring-steel build sheet. 
2.  **Magnetic Alignment Check:** Orient your Neodymium magnets. Ensure the left mating face presents absolute **North polarity outward**, and the reciprocating right face presents **South polarity outward**.
3.  **Localized Press-Fit:** Support the cored side tabs from underneath using an assembly block. Use your manual arbor press to drive the 6mm x 2mm discs flush into their retention slots. **Do not apply vertical downward clamping pressure across the hollow unsupported top face.**
4.  **Badge Ingestion:** Slide the 100% dense metallic gold PETG brand badge down into the front keyway track until it clicks into its base lock position.

---

## 📊 SECTION 2: LABORATORY BENCH CALIBRATION & DROP TESTING

Every production run must clear this cored impact check to guarantee that dropping the mass didn't weaken the structural wall intersections.

### The Loaded Matrix Drop Verification
*   **SOP:** Load all 12 cartridge slots with solid steel testing cylinders. Raise the fully weighted, shelled tray to a height of **1.5 meters (approx. 5 feet)** over a solid concrete workshop floor. Release the tray to force a flat-face base impact landing.
*   **Failure Metrics:** The internal 2.0mm vertical support column webs must absorb the impact shock cleanly. The unit must show **zero internal column cracking, zero perimeter wall splits, and zero structural warping**. The side magnetic interlock tabs must remain 100% rigid. Any physical deformation or part ejection triggers an immediate batch **FAILURE**, and the run is routed straight to the shredder matrix to be re-compounded.
