### A. The Production Labor Resolution (Automated Bed Clearing)
*   **The Reality Check:** Running a 3-printer farm at a 68% active operating duty cycle generates 66 units a day, requiring manual workspace interventions at unpredictable morning hours to clear beds and strip layer lines.
*   **The Solution:** We configure your print plates as a single, multi-object unified workspace block. By utilizing a **0.4mm Hardened Steel Nozzle Matrix** and printing supportless flat faces directly on textured PEI spring-steel sheets, parts auto-release as the bed drops below 35°C. Your manual intervention footprint is throttled back to a single 15-minute facility workspace clearance run every 24 hours.

### B. Material Purity and Sourcing Continuity
*   **The Reality Check:** Sourcing consistent post-consumer recycled polymer strands that are completely free of microscopic dust or volatile humidity bubbles is highly volatile. A single contaminated spool will clog a print head halfway through a 7-hour cycle.
*   **The Solution:** Our facility processing manual (`operations.md`) implements a strict **Dynamic Hotend Purge Routine** and mandates storage inside solid-state dehumidification vaults ($\le 20\%$ relative humidity). Furthermore, your initial 90-day operational runway completely relies on certified, premium third-party recycled pellets mixed with a constant **25% virgin weather-proof pellet feedstock** to fully protect extrusion volumetric speeds from raw material degradation.

### C. The METRC Regulatory Strategy (The Phase 1 Focus Pivot)
*   **The Reality Check:** Attempting to slide filled oil cartridges out of failed disposable devices directly on an active automated filling line triggers extreme state compliance tracking audits and cross-contamination risks under strict METRC laws.
*   **The Solution:** To guarantee absolute safety, **the company completely benches all regulated hardware components on Day One.** The Air Scrubber, the Gutter Tree, the Airless Tire, and the Vape Enclosure are fully frozen inside your private repository files. 100% of your farm's initial manufacturing capacity is strictly restricted to **Product Variant 5: The MKX Modular Retail Matrix Display Tray**. Because a display tray carries flat-zero regulatory METRC risk, uses zero electronic circuitry, and requires zero medical packaging inspections, it serves as the ultimate risk-free pilot to validate your foundry’s real-world turnaround speeds.
