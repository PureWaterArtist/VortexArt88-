## 🛠️ SECTION 1: THE PASSION-FLOWER UNIVERSAL INTERLOCKING TIMELINE

Every product in the Floral Biomimetic collection incorporates a standardized, central hexagonal female connector keyway. This allows your family to configure, stack, and interchange the modules toollessly using our 3-step manual process:

### 1. Anti-Pneumatic Alignment Slide
*   **Action:** Take the central star-shaped Passion-Flower Universal Key Spline. Align the male hexagonal output post directly over the rear female keyway socket. Push the post straight into the core. Trapped inner air columns will instantly and silently vent out through the hard-coded 0.8mm air-bleed channel at the back of the socket, allowing a fluid, zero-resistance connection with flat-zero pneumatic spring back.

### 2. The 90-Degree Expansion T-Lock
*   **Action:** Rotate the central turn-dial clockwise exactly 90 degrees by hand. The male splines expand smoothly against the interior walls of the rubber cavity, forming an ironclad, rattle-free friction-lock that can support over 25 pounds of heavy vertical weight load without a single metal screw or strip of tape.

### 3. The 45-Degree Angle and Bed-Cooling Rules
*   **The Carnation Hinge Check:** To guarantee lifetime resilience across our Carnation bag clips, the parts must always slice rotated exactly 45 degrees along the build sheet grid inside the software. This forces the toolhead to weave cross-hatched paths over the living crease line, permanently stopping layer boundary tears.
*   **The Sub-35°C Cooling Loop:** Never rip fresh TPU or PP parts off the bed immediately after a print finishes. Build plates must cool completely below 35°C before extraction. This allows the polymer lattices to naturally stabilize to spec dimensions, letting components slide apart toollessly without rubber distortion.
