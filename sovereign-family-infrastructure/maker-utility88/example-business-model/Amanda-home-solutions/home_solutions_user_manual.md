## 🛠️ SECTION 1: THE PASSION-FLOWER UNIVERSAL INTERLOCKING TIMELINE

Every product in the Floral Biomimetic collection incorporates a standardized, central hexagonal female connector keyway. This allows your family to configure, stack, and interchange the modules toollessly using our 4-step manual process:

### 1. The Mechanical T-Spline Sleeve Infiltration
*   **Action:** Take your rigid Polypropylene Orchid Under-Sink Caddy body and your flat-printed soft 85A TPU caddy grip sleeves. Roll each flexible TPU sleeve into a soft cylinder by hand with the male T-splines facing outward. 
*   **The Insertion:** Slide the male splines straight down into the caddy’s internal vertical female slots. The cross-sectional T-slot geometry locks the sleeve permanently against peeling forces. This completely bypasses chemical multi-material over-molding failures, ensuring a lifetime structural friction fit that will never delaminate under heavy spray bottle strikes.

### 2. The Flower Tower Nesting Extraction
*   **Action:** When unboxing a newly arrived Flower Tower shipping tube collection, grip the upper rim of the nested Peony prep container and pull upward vertically. 
*   **The Zero-Drag Kinetic Action:** Because our design hardcodes a 1-degree conical draft angle taper along the vertical outer Z-axis wall loops, a lifting movement of just 1.0mm instantly shatters all surface-to-surface rubber friction drag between the rigid Polypropylene container and the soft 85A TPU caddy lining. Contact area drops flatly to zero, allowing the nested layers to glide out of one another toollessly with absolute zero sticking, locking, or vacuum pocket resistance.

### 3. The Asymmetrical Fluid-Drainage Gutter Slide
*   **Action:** Take the central star-shaped Passion-Flower Universal Key Spline. Align the male hexagonal output post directly over the rear female keyway socket. Push the post straight into the core. If the caddy or wall pod is wet from washing, trapped fluid and air flash out sideways through the four 1.0mm V-groove drainage gutters on the socket floor.

### 4. The 90-Degree Expansion T-Lock
*   **Action:** Rotate the central turn-dial clockwise exactly 90 degrees by hand. The male splines expand smoothly against the interior walls of the rubber cavity, forming an ironclad, rattle-free friction-lock that supports 25 pounds of weight without a single metal screw or strip of tape.
