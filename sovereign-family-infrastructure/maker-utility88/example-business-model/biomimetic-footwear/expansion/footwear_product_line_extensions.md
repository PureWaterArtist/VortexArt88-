Because our direct-to-consumer drops are driven by code, we can implement high-value customizations that traditional mass-production factories cannot mathematically replicate:

1.  **On-Demand Logo Overmolding:** We can embed custom text strings, regional club branding, or local sports team logos directly into the rear vertical heel-latch toggle faceplate within the OpenSCAD design tree.
