## 🛠️ SECTION 1: SYSTEM CLOSED-LOOP RE-ENGINEERED ASSEMBLY TIMELINE

Because this footwear architecture utilizes a modular, 3-piece toolless configuration to bypass multi-material travel stringing, bench processing loops are reduced to three clean manual events:

### 1. Longitudinal Dovetail Slide Insertion
*   **Action:** Take your printed soft 85A TPU upper shell and your thick 95A TPU cushion sole base. Align the three solid T-shaped male splines on the bottom of the upper shell with the three female keyway tracks on the heel of the sole.
*   **The Sliding Action:** Slide the upper shell forward lengthwise into the sole tracks until the toe box hits the front stop wall. The interlocking dovetail geometry mechanically blocks vertical pulling forces and twisting walking shear completely, while allowing your team to slide the parts apart backward in under 5 seconds for zero-tear recycling extraction.

### 2. Velvet Comfort Insert Infiltration
*   **Action:** Slide the hyper-thin 2.0mm velvet-matte 80A TPU insole insert flat inside the ankle collar cavity. Press it down firmly until it rests flush against the interior footbed contouring bed, sealing the underlying structural Gyroid cushion channels completely sub-surface.

### 3. Static Leak-Proof Diagnostic Audit
*   **Action:** Place the fully assembled footwear clog flat inside a 1-inch deep water testing tray for 60 seconds. Flex the toe box dynamically up to 30 degrees to simulate active stride kinematics.
*   **Evaluation Parameters:** The interior footbed insert must remain completely bone dry. The interlocking Asymmetrical Hydrophobic Tesla-Valve micro-tunnels must pass rising internal vapor pressure upward during flexion while choking off inbound hydrostatic puddle pressure spikes, ensuring absolute water isolation with flat-zero chemical glues.
