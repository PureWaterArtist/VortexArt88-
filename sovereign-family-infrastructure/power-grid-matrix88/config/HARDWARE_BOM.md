2. **Coanda Manifold Alignment:** Bolt the Coanda-effect fluidic logic manifold plate straight to the gas exit vents, ensuring the internal 120-micron tracking lines are fully open and clear of soot blocks.
3. **Stirling Engine Coupling:** Fit the mechanical Stirling engine cylinder directly over the alternating thermal exhaust ports, securing the interface with your braided graphite compression gaskets to prevent gas leaks.
4. **Thermal Pyrolysis Test Run:** Pack the screen with dry wood chips from Project Shear-Cut, light the initial starter tinder, and slide the air seal closed. Monitor the fluidic switches as they cycle gas at 15 Hz to run the mechanical generator drive in total silence without a drop of gasoline.
