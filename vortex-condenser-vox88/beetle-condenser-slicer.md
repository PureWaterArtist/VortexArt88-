🖨️ Slicing Settings for Maximum Yield

Because FDM 3D printers lay down plastic in layers, you can actually use the physical limitations of the printer to enhance this beetle effect.

Layer Height: Set your slicer to 0.28mm or 0.3mm (Coarse/Draft). Printing with thicker layers creates horizontal stair-steps all along the model. 

These microscopic layer lines act as the "hydrophilic bumps," catching tiny air-bound mist particles and channeling them downward.

Fuzzy Skin Option: If you are using Bambu Studio or PrusaSlicer, enable the "Fuzzy Skin" setting for the outer wall only. This instructs the nozzle to vibrate slightly, giving the outer shell a rough, organic texture that mimics the actual skin of the desert beetle, increasing the surface area for moisture condensation.

Perimeters: Set Wall Loops to 6. This eliminates any internal gaps in the print, ensuring the part is 100% solid and completely watertight without needing post-processing coatings.
