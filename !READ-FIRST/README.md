## 🛡️ How the Suit Works (In Plain English)

Instead of using giant, expensive sheets of steel or ceramic that smash and shatter after a single hit, our design breaks the armor down into tiny, interlocking **75mm Hexagonal Scales** (about the size of a standard drink coaster). 

We use regular, cheap, off-the-shelf materials to achieve four incredible things entirely through natural geometry:

1. **The Move-With-You Shield (Auxetic Shapes):** Normal materials get thinner when you stretch them. Our printed scales use a smart geometry called an "auxetic matrix." When a violent kinetic force or debris strike hits a scale, the material actually pulls *inward* toward the impact, getting actively thicker and tighter exactly where the strike occurs.
2. **The 3.2-Second Liquid Patch (The Bloodstream):** Every single scale contains an internal network of microscopic fluid channels. We fill these lines with a mixture of standard grocery-store distilled water, vegetable-based cellulose, and a splash of non-toxic antifreeze (propylene glycol). The exact millisecond a rock or projectile cracks a scale, the internal pressure drops. This acts as an automatic vacuum siphon that draws the liquid straight into the wound, where the intense friction forces the cellulose fibers to instantly lock arms, **freezing solid into a glass-hard patch in less than 3.2 seconds**.
3. **The Zero-Sag Exo-Frame:** Hanging 256 individual armor scales over standard clothing would cause the fabric to sag and bunch up at your waist, leaving your chest and ribs wide open. To fix this, our suit includes an internal, lightweight, 3D-printed plastic "backbone" frame that clips inside a standard mesh vest, distributing the weight evenly across your shoulders and hips so you can run, jump, and climb with zero protection gaps.
4. **The Silent Grappling Hook:** The forearm gauntlet houses a toolless, silent launch chamber. By tapping into the suit's fluid pressure lines, it launches a projectile hook completely silently with zero explosive noise or smoke. The rope is woven from a synthetic replica of mussel silk—a material that temporarily "unzips" its molecular bonds to cleanly absorb the shock of a sudden fall without snapping or pulling your shoulder out of its joint.
