## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

To execute the toolless alignment sequence, clear microfluidic internal pathways, and successfully execute your first subsoil trenching run on your workshop bench, follow these step-by-step assembly phases:

1. **Acoustic Head Integration:** Wire the 40.0 kHz piezoelectric transducer array elements directly into the front nose cone housing. Verify that the acoustic focal lines cross exactly 1.5mm ahead of the leading edge face.
2. **Peristaltic Core Installation:** Print the flexible, hollow earthworm pumping tube using your elastomeric tough resin. Ensure the internal 120-micron microfluidic logic capillaries print completely hollow and free of resin blocks.
3. **Radial Block Interlocking:** Snap the rigid PLA auxetic root-tip compression blocks around the perimeter of the peristaltic tube to assemble the multi-vessel expansion shell.
4. **Pneumatic Flow Check:** Hook your compressed air lines to the multi-chamber distribution manifold. Run an initial test pass at 212.5 kPa to confirm that sequential pressure waves pulse down the elastomeric walls cleanly to activate the peristaltic stream.
