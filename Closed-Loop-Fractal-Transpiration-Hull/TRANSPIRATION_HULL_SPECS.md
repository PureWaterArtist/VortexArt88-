# Architectural Manifesto: Fractal Transpiration Hull Project

This project establishes the open-source hardware design framework for a scale-invariant, biomimetic structural housing. The design utilizes macro-scale geometric branching profiles to optimize fluid transport efficiency and structural rigidity.

## Core Architectural Constraints
1. **Geometric Self-Similarity:** The housing utilizes a Constructal Law branching ratio to minimize fluid drag across structural intersections.
2. **Macro-Scale Fluid Conduits:** All fluid and air transport pathways within the primary printable chassis must maintain a minimum physical cross-section of 4.0mm to ensure mechanical sanitization compliance and prevent layer-line clogging.
3. **FDM Printing Parity:** The structural walls are optimized for continuous toolpath perimeters (4 to 6 walls) to enforce watertight sealing natively from the slicer.
4. **Interfacial Versatility:** The base frame features a universal mechanical flange configuration designed to mount directly onto standard industrial containers and storage barrels.
