This document establishes the hard-locked open-source hardware specifications, metrology constants, and biomimetic design criteria for the Fractal Transpiration Hull project. The design utilizes macro-scale geometric constraints to optimize passive fluid transport efficiency, structural loading distribution, and manufacturing reliability.

## 🐊 Section 2: Integrated Macro-Biomimetic Processes

To maximize fluidic capability and mechanical durability entirely through shape configuration, the engine integrates four distinct, macro-scale biological processes:

### 1. External Stenocara Beetle Ripples
*   **Geometry:** 30 concentric horizontal macro-ridges ($1.5\text{ mm}$ protrusion height) cascading down the exterior slope.
*   **Function:** Displaces the laminar boundary layer of incoming ambient wind, creating localized low-pressure drop points that force micro-mist particles to rapidly coalesce into heavy droplets.

### 2. Internal Texas Horned Lizard Sluices
*   **Geometry:** 36 vertical, open-faced internal drainage tracks ($3.0\text{ mm}$ wide at base, tapering to $1.0\text{ mm}$ at apex, $1.0\text{ mm}$ deep).
*   **Function:** Utilizes gravity-driven fluid routing to accelerate downward draining. The wide, open profile prevents the capillary water-locking associated with microfluidic paths, allowing for rapid mechanical cleaning and complete aeration.

### 3. Structural Bamboo Nodal Rings
*   **Geometry:** 6 interior horizontal structural bulkheads ($3.0\text{ mm}$ inward wall protrusion, $6.0\text{ mm}$ thickness) spaced evenly along the vertical axis.
*   **Function:** Mimics the internal nodal distribution of bamboo stalks. Drastically multiplies resistance to crushing and bending forces without increasing overall solid weight, while acting as a passive fluid velocity dampener.

### 4. Logarithmic Nautilus Guide Vanes
*   **Geometry:** 4 sweeping, 5mm-thick macro-curved vanes situated within the bottom 40.0mm of the intake throat, conforming to a 45-degree sweeping logarithmic spiral arc.
*   **Function:** Scoops incoming ambient air currents and pre-rotates the mass into a steady, hyper-laminar vortex before it enters the compression core, significantly reducing intake turbulence and backpressure.

## 🖨️ Section 3: Manufacturing Compliance Rules

To maintain safe, unassailable open-source status and ensure structural success during 3D fabrication, all physical models must adhere to the following rules:

1.  **Macro-Scale Clearance:** All fluid paths, channels, and structural clearances must maintain a strict minimum dimension of 1.0mm. Sub-millimeter microfluidics are forbidden to eliminate biological pathogen trapping and biofilm preservation risks.
2.  **Continuous Toolpathing:** Walls must be printed using a minimum of 4 to 6 continuous perimeter loops to eliminate internal infill voids and render the part physically watertight.
3.  **Self-Supporting Taper:** The primary conic geometry must maintain a structural overhang angle capable of printing completely upright without the use of temporary internal breakaway support materials.
