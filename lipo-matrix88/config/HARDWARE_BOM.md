## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

1. **Trabeculae Chassis Casting:** Cast or 3D-print the open-cell bone-biomimetic structural housing shell using non-flammable silicon-carbide slurry. Slow-bake in a pottery kiln to secure structural density.
2. **Eel Plaque Layering:** Stack the porous carbon graphene plates inside the 75mm regular hexagonal scale chambers. Ensure the flexible copper-nanowire terminal busbars fit tightly to achieve low internal electrical resistance.
3. **Cooling Track Alignment:** Seat the flexible elastomeric cooling jacket lines containing the hidden 120-micron microfluidic tracking channels completely flush between the electro-plaque rows.
4. **Capacitance Surge Test:** Hook your inputs directly to the Project Solar-Armor array lines. Run a peak daylight charging surge to verify that the supercapacitor matrix accepts a full load in under 45.0 seconds, while monitoring the 200 kPa water jackets as they stabilize the cell cores at a safe, flat 25°C operating equilibrium.
