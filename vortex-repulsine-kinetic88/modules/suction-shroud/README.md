## 🌪️ System Manifest & Inward Symmetrical Mass Airflow Philosophy

The **Dorsal Suction Shroud Module (Project Repulsine-Shroud)** houses the aerodynamic intake inlet tolerances, coaxial fan geometries, and pre-charge motor limits for the 2-passenger compact prototype. Traditional aviation propulsion loops fight ambient headwind drag by extending blunt, heavy structural forward surfaces that force passing air *outward* and away from the craft. This creates massive air friction buffers that break down lifting efficiency.

This module establishes the unyielding blueprints for an active aerodynamic intake shield hull. The entire upper (dorsal) dome of the craft is transformed into a massive **Passive Centripetal Convergence Shroud** featuring a strict **5:1 volumetric compression ratio**. A high-speed, zero-contact coaxial starter fan embedded in the center hub draws ambient atmospheric air violently *inward*, pulling the entire vehicle body upward into its own low-pressure vacuum intake current. By lining the interior intake walls with a single atomic layer of **CVD Graphene**, boundary-layer drag is dropped to near-zero. This ensures the compressed air mass shoots into the lower counter-rotating spin plates with maximum kinetic momentum, sustaining the implosion cycle entirely on ambient wind currents.
