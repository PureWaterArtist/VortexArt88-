*   **Microfluidic Logic Track Width:** Exactly $250.0\text{ \mu m}$ ($0.25\text{ mm}$ micro-etched channels).
*   **Nominal Logic Track Depth:** Exactly $250.0\text{ \mu m}$ (Perfect 1:1 cross-sectional aspect ratio).

*   **Vortex Ignition Velocity:** 12,500.0 RPM baseline.
*   **Fluid Logic Transit Velocity:** Under transmitter stick deflection, the water stream switches tracks at a rapid **$3.82\text{ m/s}$ baseline rate**.
