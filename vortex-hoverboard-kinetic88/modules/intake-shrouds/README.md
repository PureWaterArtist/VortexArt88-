## 🌪️ System Manifest & Inward Passive Airflow Siphoning Philosophy

The **Perimeter Intake Shrouds Module (Project Skate-Shroud)** houses the aerodynamic slot tolerances, micro-funnel inlet curves, and boundary tracking channels for the personal hoverboard deck. Traditional wheeled transport systems battle ambient headwind resistance by riding directly through the air column, creating high-pressure drag waves beneath and around the platform that degrade velocity and balance.

This module establishes the blueprints for a passive aerodynamic air-siphoning deck edge. The entire outer perimeter rim of the hoverboard deck is carved into an array of downward-sloping **Perimeter Venturi Slots** featuring a strict **3:1 volumetric compression ratio**. The high-speed centripetal suction generated by the underside counter-rotating spin plates pulls ambient atmospheric air violently *inward* through these slots, continuously harvesting the surrounding air mass. By lining the internal micro-funnel walls with an atomic layer of **CVD Graphene**, boundary-layer friction drag is dropped to near-zero. This ensures the compressed air mass shoots into the intake manifold paths with maximum kinetic momentum, feeding the ground-effect air cushion entirely on passing wind profiles with zero electrical overhead.
