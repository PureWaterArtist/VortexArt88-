## 📸 Technical Asset Verification & Cleanroom Camera Protocols

To maintain absolute data integrity and verifiable prior-art across all open-source submissions to the public ledger, all laser-interferometer snapshots, material line traces, or airflow latency records uploaded here by the community must strictly adhere to these formatting rules:

1.  **High-Contrast Slot Renders:** All CAD or computational fluid dynamics (CFD) exports must use a clean, dark-mode background profile to ensure that the 3:1 volumetric tapering lines are perfectly scannable on workshop monitors.
2.  **Slot Aperture Micro-Documentation:** High-resolution photography demonstrating successful deposition of the atomic graphene layer across the internal slot funnel perimeters must be shot under macro edge focus to check for structural anomalies.
3.  **Mass Flow Air Speed Logs:** Visual data overlays tracking the 2.8 kg/s air mass capture rate and the 45-degree stable banking limits must link directly back to the parameters calculated inside the root `master-skate-twin.py` digital twin script.
