## 🚫 The Problem With Digital Remote Controls

Modern electric skateboards or self-balancing hoverboards rely completely on wireless handheld Bluetooth remotes, digital computer software chips, and electronic gyroscopes to decide when to accelerate or brake. This design introduces three dangerous vulnerabilities: if the remote's Bluetooth signal drops out due to local radio interference, if the digital chip crashes, or if water shorts out the gyroscope circuitry, the board can lock its brakes or shut off completely, throwing the rider onto the hard pavement.
