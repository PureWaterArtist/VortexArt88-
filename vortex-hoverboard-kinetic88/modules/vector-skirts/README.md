## 🎛️ System Manifest & Software-Free Directional Vectoring Philosophy

The **Geometric Fluidic Logic Flight Vector Module (Project Skate-Vector)** houses the micro-channel track configurations, 30-degree bistable jet deflection angles, and autonomous hydraulic detour parameters for the personal all-terrain deck. Traditional electric skateboards or mobility devices rely on mechanical trucks, physical wheel friction turning, or complex electronic speed controllers to execute braking. If an operator encounters high-velocity speed wobbles or severe surface oil, a physical wheel can slip, causing an immediate loss of control.

This directory details the blueprints for a completely software-free, fluidic traction envelope. The exhaust exits of the dual front-and-rear lifting cells lead directly into curved deflection tracks precision-etched into the deck edges and lined with an atomic layer of hydrophobic **CVD Graphene**. When the rider shifts their posture, the altered fluid volume split vectors thrust instantly through the **Coandă Effect**, forcing the high-velocity stream to naturally adhere to the selected geometric curve. If the operator leans hard into a carve or shifts weight backward to decelerate, the water loops split evenly or reverse paths, executing a **Hydraulic Inversion Counter-Vortex** that acts as an invisible, friction-free magnetic brake. This ensures absolute stability at a rapid **3.82 m/s fluid transit velocity** with a strict $0.00\text{ ms}$ capillary logic lag.
