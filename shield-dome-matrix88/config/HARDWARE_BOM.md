## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

1. **Knuckle and Strut Frame Lock:** 3D-print the auxetic knuckle hubs using your high-stiffness composite filament. Bolt the pre-stressed basalt-fiber tension rods tightly to the knuckles to assemble the geodesic skeleton web framework, validating that the backing ratio reads a crisp -0.60.
2. **Helicoidal Scale Printing:** 3D-print the 120mm regular hexagonal shroud scales using your zinc-infused tough resin slurry. Ensure the internal 4.5mm sinusoidal micro-trusses are completely open and clean of blocked artifacts.
3. **Venting Seam Alignment:** Interlock the completed shroud scales over the basalt strut skeleton, using the 2.4mm flexible polyurethane edge gaskets. Verify that the hidden 120-micron Coanda fluidic overpressure logic chutes are clear.
4. **Pneumatic Overpressure Test:** Blow an high-velocity pressure stream across the scale borders to confirm that the valveless fluidic switches activate within 0.36 seconds, venting the blast stream harmlessly up and over the canopy apex without buckling the structural knuckles.
