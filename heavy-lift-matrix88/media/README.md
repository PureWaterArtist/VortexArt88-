## 📸 Technical Asset Verification & Cleanroom Camera Protocols

To maintain absolute data integrity and verifiable prior-art across all open-source submissions to the public trust ledger, all laser-interferometer snapshots, layer deposition logs, or pressure latency records uploaded here by the community must strictly adhere to these formatting rules:

1.  **High-Contrast Core Renders:** All CAD or fluid-duct schematic exports must use a clean, dark-mode background profile to ensure that the valveless pneumatic hydrostat channels and pre-stressed banyan tendons are perfectly scannable on workshop monitors.
2.  **Pressure-Delta Micro-Documentation:** High-resolution magnification photography demonstrating successful printing of the flexible polymer layers and their corresponding 120-micron microfluidic internal capillaries must be shot under macro edge focus to check for channel anomalies.
3.  **Hydrostatic Load Logs:** Visual data overlays tracking the 212.5 kPa nominal working input pressure and the strict 8,500-Newton gecko suction anchor force window must link directly back to the parameters calculated inside the root `simulate-lift-performance.py` simulation engine.
