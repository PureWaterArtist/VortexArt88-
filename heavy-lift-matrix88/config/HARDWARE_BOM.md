## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

To execute the toolless alignment sequence, clear microfluidic internal pathways, and successfully execute your first 300 kg mass lift on your workshop bench, follow these step-by-step assembly phases:

1. **Hydrostat Arm Curing:** Print the 1,500mm flexible hydrostat arm using elastomeric photopolymer resin, verifying that the parallel, nested 120-micron internal air capillaries print fully hollow and un-clogged.
2. **Banyan Frame Interlocking:** Snap the 3D-printed auxetic structural banyan-core blocks together. Thread your synthetic mussel-silk tendon lines tightly through the star-cell guide loops to pre-stress the main truss skeleton.
3. **Gecko Base Bonding:** Adhere your Carbon Nanotube (MWCNT) dry-adhesion setae sheets firmly to the $400\text{mm} \times 400\text{mm}$ base plate scales. Attach your differential siphon valves to supply the mandated +2.5 kPa vacuum lock lock.
4. **Pneumatic Loop Priming:** Connect your pressurized air manifold to the 120-micron capillaries. Run an initial test pass at 212.5 kPa to confirm that the arm flexes and rigidifies natively through fluid logic in total silence with zero oil leaks.
