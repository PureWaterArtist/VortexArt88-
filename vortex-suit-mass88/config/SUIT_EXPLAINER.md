## 🚫 The Bottleneck: Why Old-World Body Armor Cannot Be Mass Produced

Traditional military bulletproof vests or tactical armor suits are manufactured using giant, multi-ton hydraulic presses that stamp out massive single-piece steel plates or ceramic sheets. This setup costs hundreds of thousands of dollars, restricting the production of body armor to massive state-funded defense factories. If a regular citizen wants protection, they are completely locked out by price gouging and artificial supply chain shortages.

**PROJECT RESO-SUIT breaks this corporate enclosure by shrinking the armor into tiny, mass-printable hexagonal scales.**

## ⚡ The Step-by-Step Production Sequence Explained

Instead of trying to print a giant, complex chest plate, our mass-production framework uses a simple, highly efficient strategy:

1. **Bulk Printing the Scales:** The entire suit is made out of a single, standardized part: an **8mm-thin, 75mm-wide Hexagonal Scale**. Because the piece is tiny, you can pack dozens of them onto the build plate of a basic, low-cost desktop resin 3D printer. A single home workshop can churn out over 100 scales every 24 hours for pennies on the dollar.
2. **The 0.5mm Click Interlock:** Each printed scale features tiny, built-in snap clips along its outer edges. We built a precise **0.5mm clearance gap** straight into the design code. This means even if your home 3D printer has minor imperfections, the scales will still click together smoothly like Lego pieces, snapping directly onto any standard heavy-duty mesh shirt or jacket.
3. **The Interconnected Bloodstream:** Every single hex scale contains an internal vascular network of microscopic **120-micron channels**. When you click the scales together on your suit garment, their vertical fluid ports line up automatically. Small, inexpensive rubber O-rings sit between the scales, sealing the joints and merging hundreds of separate tiles into a single, continuous, full-body vascular loop.
4. **The Fluid-Driven Defense:** You fill the suit's network using a standard plastic syringe loaded with our low-cost water-and-cellulose mixture. If a high-velocity kinetic strike hits your arm or torso, the unique auxetic shape of the scale forces the material to violently pull *inward*, tightening and thickening exactly where the strike occurs. The moment the casing cracks, the local pressure drops, vacuum-siphoning the fluid straight into the wound to **solidify and seal the crack into a glass-hard patch in under 3.2 seconds**. 

Makers can print the exact same part over and over again, connecting as many scales as needed to build a custom fit for any body height. It is a completely open, un-hackable, and self-repairing shield built entirely by the community, for the community.
