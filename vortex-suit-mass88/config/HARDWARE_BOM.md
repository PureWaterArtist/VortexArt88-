## 🏭 Workshop Mass Production Slicing Staging Protocol

To maximize your home workbench output and print your 256 required hexagonal scales with flawless capillary tolerances, enforce these exact settings inside your desktop slicer software (e.g., Chitubox, Lychee, or PrusaSlicer):
1. **Layer Orientation:** Lay the hexagonal scales completely flat on the print bed surface. This aligns the 120-micron internal micro-capillaries parallel to your LCD layer lines, completely eliminating sagging resin blocks.
2. **Vat Exposure Tuning:** Set your initial bottom layer exposure to 35 seconds (4 layers) to guarantee absolute build-plate adhesion. Set your normal layer exposure to exactly 2.4 seconds (based on a standard monochrome mono-LCD light engine).
3. **Capillary Purge Routine:** Immediately upon print completion, submerge the scales inside an ultrasonic denatured alcohol bath. Inject clean alcohol straight through the center vertical port using your Luer-Lock syringe to completely flush out trapped liquid resin before it cures under UV workspace ambient lights.
