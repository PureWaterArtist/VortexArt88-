## 🖨️ Step 1: Bulk Desktop Printing & Capillary Flushing
1. **Slicer Orientation:** Orient your 75mm hexagonal scales completely flat on the print bed inside your slicing software. This ensures that the internal 120-micron micro-capillary tracks run parallel to your printer's LCD light engine, eliminating internal track warping or resin sagging faults.
2. **Exposure Tuning:** Set your initial bottom layers exposure to 35 seconds (4 layers) to guarantee absolute build-plate adhesion. Set your normal layer exposure to exactly 2.4 seconds on any monochrome LCD screen.
3. **Vascular Purge Routine:** Immediately upon print completion, pop the scales off the bed and submerge them in an ultrasonic bath of denatured isopropyl alcohol. Use your Luer-Lock syringe to flush clean alcohol straight through the ports, clearing uncured liquid resin out of the capillaries before it bakes solid under ambient workshop lights.

## 🧩 Step 2: Mesh Garment Clapping & O-Ring Interlocking
1. **Base Mesh Preparation:** Lay your off-the-shelf mesh tactical vest and limb sleeves flat on an open workbench table.
2. **EPDM Gasket Placement:** Insert a standard 1.2mm inner-diameter EPDM rubber O-ring into the precision shelf groove cut around the 120-micron fluid port of each scale component.
3. **The Click-Interlock:** Position the scales side-by-side and snap their built-in mechanical edge clips together over the mesh fabric lines. The 0.5mm built-in clearance gap provides an airtight, press-fit alignment hold that automatically connects the 120-micron capillaries across the entire suit array, forming a single continuous vascular grid around your limbs and torso.

## 💧 Step 3: Compounding & Vacuum-Injecting the Bloodstream
1. **The Formula:** Pour 2 Gallons of distilled grocery water into a clean container. Slowly disperse high-aspect-ratio Crystalline Nanocellulose (CNC) whiskers into the liquid to establish a perfect 1.5% volumetric concentration. Stir for 5 minutes until a clear, homeostatic suspension matrix forms.
2. **Syringe Charging:** Draw the compiled fluid medium into your 50mL Luer-Lock syringe. Connect the needle directly to the primary vertical transfer ports on your torso panel scales.
3. **Vacuum Bleeding:** Inject the fluid slowly to keep the flow within the hyper-laminar regime ($Re = 0.05$). Lightly tap the printed resin tiles with a plastic tool to drive out internal air bubbles and micro-pockets. Once the entire full-body loop is full, clamp the joint ports tightly using a drop of waterproof rubber adhesive.
