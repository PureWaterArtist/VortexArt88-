## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

1. **Shark-Skin Shell Printing:** 3D-print the multi-layered concentric hex-grid shell using optical tough resin. Ensure the sub-micron mako riblet textures and internal 120-micron gill lamellae tracking lines are perfectly molded to maximize hydrodynamic drag reduction.
2. **Enzymatic Core Layering:** Pack the interior exhalation manifold with the zinc-co-catalyzed carbonic anhydrase gel matrix. Verify that the concentric EPDM thermal reclaim sleeves wrap tightly around the manifold paths to lock the breath heat exchange loop.
3. **Mouthpiece Coupling:** Snap the valveless Coanda-effect mouthpiece manifold straight over the inhalation and exhalation ports, checking that the 120-micron capillaries are entirely open and clear of resin blocks.
4. **Hydro-Thermal Breathing Test:** Hook your inputs to a low-power compressed air line and drop the unit into a 4°C sub-zero water tank. Apply breath waves to verify the valveless Coanda manifold splits flow natively at 10 kPa, while the core pre-heats the air loop back to 24°C without ice clogging.
