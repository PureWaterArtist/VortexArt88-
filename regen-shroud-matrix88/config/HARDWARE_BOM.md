## 🔬 Calibration & Assembly Staging Protocol (The First Launch Setup)

1. **Extracellular Scaffold Extrusion:** Blend exactly 45.0% by mass of the high-purity chitosan powder with 55.0% hyaluronic acid liquid. 3D-print the open-pore tissue scaffold grid, validating that the 120-micron internal vascular tracking lines print completely hollow and un-bent.
2. **Stasis Fluid Blending:** Progressively disperse the fumed silica nanoparticles into the liquid PEG-200 carrier medium at a strict 55% weight ratio. Mix under high-shear mechanical agitation until the viscoelastic slurry reaches a uniform, clear gel state.
3. **Hexagonal Scale Charging:** Injection-mold or print the 75mm clear hexagonal armor scale cells using tough resin. Vacuum-fill the scale voids with the blended Silica-PEG fluid gel, and seal the fill ports using high-strength clear resin epoxy.
4. **Shear-Locking Slam Test:** Interlock the completed scale array onto the inverted cardioid auxetic frame backing layer. Run a high-velocity impact test pass (striking a test cell at a shear strain rate of $\geq 1,200\text{ s}^{-1}$). Confirm that the scale face instantly hardens to a rigid crystalline boundary wall ($\geq 85.0\text{ Shore D}$) inside 0.02 seconds, while automatically remaining soft and flexible during slow, low-velocity mechanical movements.
