3. MECHANICAL SEALING & VACUUM PURGE SEQUENCING

- THE COPPER CRUSH GASKET INTERFACE:
  * Do not use rubber, Teflon, or silicone seals (they dry rot within decades).
  * Machined from a pure, oxygen-free dead-soft electronic copper ring (C10100) 
    measuring 102.0mm O.D. x 1.5mm Thickness.
  * The Titanium top cap is threaded using fine-pitch industrial threads. When 
    torqued down to **120 ft-lbs**, the copper ring is permanently crushed into 
    the metal grain structure, forming an absolute hermetic metal-to-metal barrier.

- ATMOSPHERIC EVACUATION MATRIX:
  1. Stack your laser-etched fused quartz plates inside the titanium chamber, 
     separating each plate with a thin sheet of pure, un-inked cotton rag paper.
  2. Thread the top cap on loosely, leaving the copper gasket partially uncompressed.
  3. Place the entire assembly inside an air-gapped vacuum purge chamber. Draw 
     down the internal atmosphere to **0.3 Pascals** to clear all ambient moisture.
  4. Flood the chamber with 99.99% pure, anhydrous **Argon or Nitrogen Gas** 
     until the pressure settles at exactly **1.2 atmospheres**.
  5. Torque the cap to its final 120 ft-lbs rating while under the gas blanket, 
     locking in a positive-pressure, bone-dry atmosphere that prevents oxidation.

4. SOIL BURIAL AND DEPLOYMENT RULES
To guarantee the capsule survives geological shifts, execute these protocols:
* BURIAL DEPTH: Minimum **2.0 Meters (6.5 Feet)** down, placing the unit safely 
                below the seasonal frost line to eliminate freeze-thaw stress.
* BACKFILL MEDIUM: Surround the titanium shell with a 150mm thick layer of 
                   washed, high-purity crystalline quartz sand. This creates 
                   a high-drainage, non-acidic buffer zone that isolates the capsule.
